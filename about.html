About Me

I'm an illustrator from Metro-Detroit who specializes in character illustration and portraiture, but I also have experience designing backgrounds, props, and products. I have much experience with digital illustration, but these days I prefer making art that I can put my hands on- primarily graphite & mixed media. After graduating from the College for Creative Studies in 2025, I am pursuing freelance illustration and tattooing.

I get my main inspiration for drawing from the online artist community I grew up with and the world around me. My favorite hobby is going on hikes with my DSLR camera to take reference photos for my collection.
